Methodology

How the calculation works

The calculator uses the Crossed-Cylinder vector method to determine the optimal toric IOL axis and power, accounting for pre-operative corneal astigmatism and surgically induced astigmatism.

1

Enter Pre-Op Keratometry

Input the flat and steep K readings from your keratometry measurement, along with their respective axes (0–180°).

2

Set SIA & Incision

Enter your Surgically Induced Astigmatism value and the incision location in degrees. Typical SIA is 0.3–0.7 D.

3

Get Axis & Cylinder

The calculator applies the Crossed-Cylinder method and returns the optimal axis of placement and the closest available cylinder model.
