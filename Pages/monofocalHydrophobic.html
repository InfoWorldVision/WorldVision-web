Monofocal · Aspheric
WVO ACE
Hydrophobic Acrylic Foldable Aspheric 360° Square Edge Intraocular Lens
WVL602SQ WVL602SQP Preloaded WVM606SQ WVM606SQP Preloaded WVL621 WVL621P Preloaded
WVO ACE is an advanced Monofocal IOL with a negative aspheric optic design. It offers many advantages compared to a normal monofocal IOL.
A negative spherical aberration of -0.20µm to compensate the average corneal aberration of most people. The transfer of light is very close to the Ideal IOL.
An improvement of the depth of focus and contrast sensitivity, for a better vision.
A better vision in case of decentering and tilting of the lens. A decentering of 0.3mm and a tilting of 0.5° don't affect much the performance of the intraocular lens.
Aspheric optic
360° Square edge
High contrast

<0.5% water

WVO ZEN
Yellow Hydrophobic Acrylic Foldable Aspheric 360° Square Edge Intraocular Lens
WVL602SQY WVL602SQYP Preloaded
WVO ZEN is the equivalent of WVO ACE with a Blue Light Filter. 10% Cut-off is at 489nm. It is an advanced Monofocal IOL with a negative aspheric optic design offering the same advantages as WVO ACE.
A negative spherical aberration of -0.20µm to compensate the average corneal aberration of most people. The transfer of light is very close to the Ideal IOL.
An improvement of the depth of focus and contrast sensitivity, for a better vision.
A better vision in case of decentering and tilting. A decentering of 0.3mm and a tilting of 0.5° don't affect much the performance of the intraocular lens.

ISO Certified Quality

All WVO monofocal lenses undergo rigorous biocompatibility testing to ISO 10993 medical device standards before leaving our facility.

USA Medical Grade Acrylic

Our hydrophobic acrylic polymer is sourced exclusively from certified USA medical-grade suppliers — the foundation of all three WVO monofocal models.

Zero Glistening

With less than 0.5% water content, WVO monofocal IOLs are completely free of glistening — preserving optical clarity throughout the patient's lifetime.
